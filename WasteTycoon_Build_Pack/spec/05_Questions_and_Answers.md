# Questions & Answers captured from planning

This section lists key “would-have-to-ask” questions and their answers, so a developer can build without needing follow‑ups.

## Game mode
- Q: Campaign or endless?
  - A: Endless sandbox.

## Menu / navigation
- Q: What is the exact menu order?
  - A: Map / Contracts / Dispatch / Fleet / Shop / Facilities / Marketplace / Help.

## Start date
- Q: What date does a new game start?
  - A: 1 Jan 2026.

## Map approach
- Q: Real UK map or stylised?
  - A: Real UK map using OSM tiles via Leaflet.

## Procedural sites
- Q: How should industrial estates work?
  - A: They yield either Depot OR Yard, never both; seeded per save.
- Q: Mechanic garages?
  - A: Mechanic garages appear in industrial estates.
- Q: Quarries?
  - A: Buyable.
- Q: Offices?
  - A: Not in housing.
- Q: Retail parks?
  - A: Can have building supply stores.

## First base gating
- Q: What must the first base be?
  - A: Transport Depot (Small) from industrial-estate depot sites.
- Q: Cost?
  - A: £50,000.

## Contracts
- Q: Deadlines?
  - A: No deadlines.
- Q: Cancel/fail?
  - A: Allowed; applies cash penalty + reputation hit.

## Staff
- Q: Drivers required?
  - A: Yes.
- Q: Licence gating?
  - A: No.
- Q: Work rules?
  - A: 15h/day max, 10h rest after.
- Q: Wage frequency?
  - A: Monthly.

## Fleet
- Q: Condition and dispatch rules?
  - A: Condition wears per mile; cannot dispatch below 10%.
- Q: Repairs?
  - A: Require mechanic access; take time.
- Q: Containers?
  - A: Individual units; no condition.

## Billing
- Q: Running costs billing schedule?
  - A: Monthly; midnight on 1st; first bill 1 Feb 2026.

## Loans
- Q: Type of loan system?
  - A: Single credit line.
- Q: Payment schedule?
  - A: Weekly flexible with £500 minimum; monthly interest.
- Q: Missed payment effect?
  - A: Reputation hit + higher interest until caught up.

## Materials and passive systems
- Q: Quarry production?
  - A: 500 t/day, evenly split; outputs Sandstone/6F2/Type1/Type2.
- Q: Building supply income?
  - A: £90k/week fixed.
- Q: How to sell materials?
  - A: Must dispatch a delivery.
- Q: Prices?
  - A: Global fixed.

## Saves
- Q: How many slots?
  - A: 5.
- Q: Autosave?
  - A: Every in-game hour.
- Q: Delete behavior?
  - A: Must type GONE; no wipe all.
